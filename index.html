I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Bo Ji. My research focuses on security and privacy in immersive and networked systems, particularly how network traffic can expose sensitive behavioral information in VR/XR.

Before joining Virginia Tech, I received an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia, an M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Ewha Womans University, and a B.S. in Software Science from Dankook University.

Motion in the Clear: Reconstructing VR User Behavior from Network Traffic

JiHo Lee, JinYi Yoon, Taejoong Chung, Brendan David-John, Bo Ji

We show that a passive network observer can reconstruct VR head and hand motion from plaintext network traffic without application access or prior knowledge of message formats, enabling privacy attacks such as user identification and virtual typing inference.
